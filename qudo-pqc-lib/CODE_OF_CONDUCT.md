# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inclusive environment for
everyone involved in the QUDO PQC project.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the project

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment of any kind
- Personal attacks or insults
- Publishing others' private information without permission

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project
maintainers at [john@zenv.ai](mailto:john@zenv.ai). All complaints will be
reviewed and investigated.
